## 1. THE MERIKARE INSCRIPTION (T1 — pending Helck glyph-pull)

**Claim:** The creator's-benefactions passage of the *Instruction for Merikare* (Papyrus Petersburg 1116A, ~lines 135–137) is the Dua Station's own operating license, to be inscribed in the original hieratic/glyphs down the **Central Channel** (the zero-point spine).

**The line (translator spread, from the archaeologicalresource.com page carrying all witnesses):**
- Tobin (2003): magic "as weapons to fend off the impact of what may come to pass"
- Lichtheim (1973): magic as weapons "to ward off the blow of events"
- Faulkner (Simpson ed.): magic as weapons "to ward off what might happen, **or dreams** by night as well as day"
- Gardiner (1914): magic as weapons "to ward off events (?), **and dreams** in the night as in the day"

**Consensus envelope (safe, cross-translator agreement):** the creator ordained *ḥkꜣ* for humankind, *as weapons*, within a governance-and-justice frame (rulers from the egg, raising the weak, striking the rebellious "as a man smites his son for his brother's sake," "God knows every name").

**Craft note:** the literal translators (Faulkner, Gardiner) preserve a **"dreams, night and day"** clause the literary translators smooth away — likely *magic as weapons to ward off the blow of events AND the [terrors of] dreams, night and day.* For operative use, restore this clause; it is better attested and squarely in the practitioner register.

**Discipline hold:** for anything inscribed in the ancient tongue, glyphs MUST come from **Helck, *Die Lehre für König Merikare* (Wiesbaden, 1988)**, not reconstructed from English witnesses (note the question marks — all four are reconstructing a damaged line). This is the difference between a real T1 element and a T1 claim resting on a website's smoothed English.

**Supporting citation (T2):** Gardiner's own footnote reads this sentence as proof of Egyptian magic as *privata religio* — "religion deliberately applied to individual and personal ends… for personal protection against accidents." A century-old Egyptologist independently confirming the sanctioned-personal-operative-power frame. Usable for the book.

**Why it belongs on the Central Channel:** the passage literally describes what the operator does standing at the lectern — wielding the faculty the creator granted to parry what comes to pass. Self-describing, in the tongue that grants it. Novel.
