## Doctrines ratified this round

**D1 — Fourteen is the frame.** Contents recombine (attested native practice, v1 F4); the count does not. Expansion, if ever, follows the attested direction only: the **Hemsut pairing** (14 male Kau + 14 feminine Hemsut = 28), logged at HORIZON as G-resonance with the mansion count of the Iah work. Not built; door noted.

**D2 — Sia's purposeful exclusion.** The fourteen Kau are *projective* endowments — powers Ra emits. Perception is *receptive* in nature and total in scope (not sight alone but apprehension entire); it is Sia's full office, not one endowment among fourteen. His absence from the roster is therefore structural, not accidental: the receiver of all cannot be one of the emitted. Tier: G/T3, internally consistent with Heru taking Seekh (#1) as *projective* intelligence-in-action. Cross-reference: Maa's directional split (#9) — downward sight seated, upward sight path-ruled — repeats the same projective/receptive grammar.

**D3 — Dual Current convergence (consistency check PASSED).** Independent seat assignment produced Het-Heru over the sustenance cluster (5, 6, 10-alt) and Heru-Behudet over the force cluster (2, 4) — recapitulating the fortunate/unfortunate poles of the Dual Current delivery mechanism (Operating Doctrine §3) without reference to it. Logged as structural convergence between the Kau layer and the delivery machinery.
