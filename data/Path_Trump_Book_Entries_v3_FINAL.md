PATH 1 — Written Covenant · The Code — Ma'at · Libra

The Mechanic
This path establishes the code that governs the installation. As the fundamental agreement that writes order into being, it sets the absolute terms under which current is permitted to flow. Without this foundational covenant, power would lack structure, boundaries would dissolve, and the circuit would fail to cohere into anything capable of manifestation. It is the scale that balances the entire machine, ensuring that every operation remains within its prescribed tolerances. The code is not a suggestion but a structural necessity; it is the law of physics applied to the spiritual circuit, dictating the precise conditions under which energy can safely cross the threshold into reality.

The Ruler
Ma'at is the exact embodiment of this path; her name and her office are identical. The presentation of Ma'at was the central temple rite across pharaonic history, affirming the core doctrine that the gods themselves live on Ma'at. The scales of judgment, where the heart is weighed against the feather of truth, operate entirely under her jurisdiction, as documented in the Book of the Dead vignettes where the deceased recites the Declaration of Innocence before the forty-two assessors. As the Old Kingdom sage Ptahhotep declared in his maxims, her order is great, lasting in effect, and unchallenged since the time of Osiris. She is the covenant that makes existence possible.

Conducting (Upright)
When the path flows, the scale balances and the agreement binds. Order is written into reality. The terms are clear, the standards are met, and the system operates in absolute equilibrium.

Faulted (Reversed)
When the path is blocked, it acts as an open circuit needing its return. The heart is not yet weighed, leaving the terms unresolved and the covenant unsigned. Justice remains pending. Work is energized without proper inspection, creating a dangerous imbalance that demands immediate rectification before true function can resume. Here stands the uncalibrated scale: power forced through a system not yet secured by truth, risking structural collapse.

Shadow-Band (Distortion)
The function inverted reveals the forty-two judges with no mercy in them. Legalism becomes a crushing tyranny, where the letter of the law is weaponized to kill the spirit. The scale is rigged from the start, and the declaration of innocence is recited merely as a hollow alibi by a system that has abandoned justice for mere compliance. When this shadow-band appears in a spread, the call is not to abandon standards but to ask whether standards still serve life. The repair begins by returning truth, proportion, and actual accountability to the code.

PATH 2 — Sequenced Images · The Sensor — Sia · Virgo

The Mechanic
This path functions as the sensory apparatus of the machine. It reads the incoming data, recognizes the patterns, and translates raw input into coherent information. Stationed at the stern, the lookout scans the channel ahead so the vessel navigates safely through the complex currents of manifestation. It is the mechanism that allows the system to perceive its environment, turning the chaos of the outside world into structured, actionable intelligence. Without this sensor, the machine is blind, incapable of responding to changes or recognizing the signs that indicate danger or opportunity.

The Ruler
Sia represents divine perception itself, standing as one of the fourteen creative powers of the cosmos. In the theology of the solar barque, Sia rides at the stern—the conscious sensor reading the environment, while Hu commands at the bow. This is perception in its purest working order, the consciousness principle that allows the divine will to apprehend its surroundings accurately. The doctrine holds that Sia is the very faculty of recognition, the power that allows the mind to sequence images into a coherent narrative of reality.

Conducting (Upright)
When the path conducts, divine perception is in perfect working order. The pattern is seen clearly, signs are read accurately, and insight arrives in its proper sequence. The barque's lookout sees the channel ahead, ensuring the current is guided by flawless situational awareness. The sensor runs at maximum efficiency, capturing the truth of the environment without distortion.

Faulted (Reversed)
When the circuit is open, the signal fails to reach the sensor. Signs are misread, perception blurs, and data arrives without meaning. The barque is left steering blind, unable to process the environment, requiring the connection to be reestablished before navigation can continue.

Shadow-Band (Distortion)
The shadow-band manifests as apophenia—the sensor manufacturing its own false order. Paranoia begins reading plots into random noise, creating a state of perception that no longer distinguishes genuine signal from the self's own paranoid projections. The lookout hallucinates monsters in the dark, steering the vessel into danger to avoid phantoms. In a spread, the correction is sober observation: separate what was actually seen from the story built around it, then let the signal establish its own sequence. Do not demand an answer from the sensor before it has received sufficient light, distance, and time to distinguish a real pattern from an anxious projection. Then act only on what the restored sensor can actually verify.

PATH 3 — Flame of Consumption · The Fuse — Sekhmet · Fire (Khepesh·Orion)

The Mechanic

It acts as a righteous force of cauterization, deploying the will to burn through what must be removed to preserve the integrity of the whole. The Ruler

The Edfu texts name her Seven Arrows, and the slaughterers at the year-turn operate under her direct command. She is the fire that purges the system of its fatal flaws, the divine immune response that attacks corruption with overwhelming force. 

Conducting (Upright)
When conducting, the purifying fire does exactly its office. The overcurrent is successfully burned off, allowing the installation to survive the surge. It is righteous force and precise cauterization—the necessary will to burn through what must go, leaving the core structure intact. The fuse blows exactly when it must, absorbing the shock and maintaining the overall integrity of the circuit.

Faulted (Reversed)
When the path is faulted, the fire will not catch, or it refuses to bank. This manifests as impotent rage, or a burn with no ground that ultimately consumes the operator. The fuse fails to blow when it should, leaving the system vulnerable to catastrophic overload. The necessary purge is delayed, allowing corruption to spread, or the anger is internalized, slowly destroying the vessel from within because the release mechanism is jammed.

Shadow-Band (Distortion)
In distortion, the Eye is entirely off the leash. It is the slaughter that only red beer could halt—wrath extending far past its commission. It becomes the purge that eats the innocent, the year-turn arrows loosed at random without purpose or restraint, a fire that seeks only to consume until nothing is left. In a spread, the signal is urgent: authority must be recalled to the flame before a necessary clearing becomes an indiscriminate devastation.

Marker note: The stellar marker Khepesh (the Foreleg) is astronomically Set's constellation, tethered by the sky-hippo; Sekhmet owns the Fire element, while the star marks the path's overcurrent-locus rather than her stellar identity.

Faulted (Reversed)

Shadow-Band (Distortion)

It is the demiurge whose perfect, flawless craft is used only to build cages, trapping rather than expressing the divine will. The creator becomes obsessed with control, using their supreme technical skill to construct a prison for the spirit. In a spread, the question is whether excellence has detached from consent and purpose; if so, the channel must be reopened to the living will it was meant to carry.

Marker note: The Orion marker (Sah/Osiris) is assigned here for the craftsman-channel mechanic, not as Ptah's attested stellar identity. Faulted (Reversed)

Shadow-Band (Distortion)

The defensive tusk is turned inward, creating a shell that never opens and ultimately suffocates what it was meant to guard. In a spread, the remedy is measured opening: re-establish the boundary, then test whether it still protects a life capable of moving, receiving, and maturing.

PATH 15 — Divine Impulse · The Spark — Auset & Atum · Libra

The Mechanic
This path is the spark of origination finding its proper vessel. Balanced initiation of current is its office: the self-kindled impulse successfully received and conceived. It requires both the point of origin and the receptacle to be perfectly aligned, endpoint answering endpoint, to ignite the system. At the precise moment of conception, ignition flashes — the masculine principle of pure energy meeting the feminine principle of form and structure. Without this perfect balance, the spark either dissipates harmlessly or destroys the vessel entirely.

The Rulers
Atum provides the spark of self-generation, the original impulse of creation named in Pyramid Texts 527. His attestation belongs to the initiating end of the circuit: the current that begins without waiting for an outside command. Atum gives this path its first movement, the impulse that makes origination possible.

Auset (Isis) provides the vessel. In the conception material, the kite conceives the heir from revived Ausar; Pyramid Texts 366 states that the seed issues into her, "she being ready as Sothis." Her attestation belongs to reception and conception, the readiness that allows the spark to become a living beginning. Together, the co-rulers form the balanced pair of origination: the two endpoints that must connect for the circuit to close. The doctrine holds that creation is never a solitary act; it requires the exact cooperation of initiator and receiver, spark and vessel, to produce manifestation.

Conducting (Upright)
When the path conducts, the true first impulse finds its vessel. The self-kindled spark is perfectly received and conceived. The seed issues into the vessel, balanced origination is achieved, and endpoint answers endpoint to start the flow. The spark catches, the fire begins to burn, and the entire system is energized by the successful union of potential and form.

Faulted (Reversed)
When the circuit is open, the impulse does not reach ground. Inspiration never conceives. It is a spark with no vessel, or a vessel with no spark, leaving origination stalled and the circuit dead. The endpoints fail to connect, and the potential remains unrealized.

Shadow-Band (Distortion)
The inverted function is impulse as compulsion. It is the ungoverned urge, creation entirely without balance. The spark scatters wildly rather than seeding, causing chaotic misfires throughout the system that damage the infrastructure without producing any viable result. In a spread, the remedy is to wait for reciprocal contact: the spark must meet a vessel that is ready, rather than forcing ignition through an unprepared field.

Marker note: The conception text naming Sothis creates a cross-lock, as the path of origination carries the system's own Sirius election inside its foundational anchor.

PATH 16 — Immersion · The Electrolyte — Tefnut · Water

Conducting (Upright)

Marker note: This Tree owns the moisture-generalized reading as doctrine. In the attestation record, however, "Tefnut = moisture" is a weakly grounded conventional gloss, while Coffin Text 80 gives her as Ma'at; the latter is carried here as a complementary facet, not a contradiction.

The Ruler

Marker note: Sirius is astronomically Sopdet/Isis's star; it is assigned to Heru here for the reliable-rising mechanic of the constant positive rail, not as his stellar identity.

PATH 18 — Eye of Matter · The Meter — Set (lower form) · Capricorn

It is the harsh but necessary assessment of actual conditions, providing the accurate data required to navigate the dense, heavy currents of the physical plane.

The Ruler
Set appears here in his lower form, representing the material nature and the Red Land. In the Amduat and Book of Gates, Set spears Apep at the prow of the solar barque, protecting the ordered cosmos. The Pyramid Texts speak of the adze made of the iron "which came forth from Set"—meteoric iron, heaven's own metal fallen to earth. The non-demonization arc of this system legitimizes this neutral materiality, recognizing that the lower nature must be accurately read and reconciled, not merely discarded. He is the master of the desert, the harsh environment that strips away illusion and forces the operator to deal with reality exactly as it is, without sentiment or self-deception.

Conducting (Upright)

Faulted (Reversed)

Shadow-Band (Distortion)

Path 18 is the lower octave that Path 11 must reconcile and raise: matter accurately seen, never worshipped and never refused.
