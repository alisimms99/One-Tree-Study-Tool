## §C — CORRECTIONS TO v1 (provenance hygiene)

1. **PT 366 ≠ "gather your limbs."** The reassembly formula is **PT 373** (§654); PT 366 is the conception episode. v1's Regeneration read implied the former under the latter's number in working notes — fixed and split across rows 14/15.
2. **"Bones of Set" = Plutarch (De Iside 62) — excluded.** The Egyptian-language basis for Set's iron is PT 21's adze-iron "which came forth from Set" + bjȝ n pt. Eye of Matter now stands on the Egyptian text only.
3. **Anubis does not wield the adze.** In the Opening of the Mouth the **sem-priest** wields it (episode 26, TT100); the Anubis-masked priest supports the mummy. The canon epithet ***The Adze*** survives on its route (the rite is the path's threshold mechanic; PT 21 supplies the instrument) — but card art and guidebook text must show Anubis presiding, not cutting.
4. **No wp-root in Anubis's name.** *jnpw* contains no *wp* element; his reckoning epithet is built on *ip* ("count"). The *wpi* ("open/divide/judge") etymology belongs to **Wepwawet only**. Any v1-era gloss linking Anubis's judging office to *wp* is struck.
5. **"Tefnut = moisture" downgraded to conventional gloss** [T2, contested]; CT 80 gives her as **Ma'at**. The card keeps the canon Elemental Water *marker* (ruling 3 governs the path, not the goddess); the book's Tefnut entry should print the honesty note.
6. **Taweret sky-hippo caveat** (Ipet/Reret labeling) printed at row 5 and §X.2.
